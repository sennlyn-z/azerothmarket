Dungeon Leveling in WoW Forever: October 2026 Beta Notes

A version-aware checklist for planning dungeon testing while XP tuning and available content continue to change.

Confirmed October 8 change

Blizzard's October 8 development notes increased experience gained from killing dungeon creatures by approximately 20%. The notes also fixed a party XP penalty issue involving higher-level players killing lower-level enemies.

Dungeon availability snapshot

  • City of Dalaran: available for testing in the October 8 notes.
  • Excavation Site: Wetlands: listed for levels 26–31 in the October 1 notes.
  • Razorfen Downs: listed as 25+ in the October 1 notes.
  • Uldaman: listed as 30+ in the October 1 notes.

How to compare XP runs

  1. Record date, build, character level and group composition.
  2. Compare similar time windows and dungeon sections rather than isolated kills.
  3. Separate official tuning from personal observations.
  4. Re-check official notes before publishing a recommended route.

These are beta snapshots, not a guarantee of launch values.
