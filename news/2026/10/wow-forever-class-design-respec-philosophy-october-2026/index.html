WoW Forever Class Design: Simple Core, More Depth, Meaningful Respecs

Blizzard says WoW: Forever will avoid wholesale modernization of Classic classes, while adding selective mechanics and keeping talent decisions meaningful.

In an interview published October 10, Blizzard described its approach as keeping classes approachable and familiar while adding depth where the original versions felt too simple or had uninteresting power swings. This is a report of developer comments, not a complete patch-note list.

Class complexity and combat pace

The team says it wants to preserve original WoW's pacing rather than make combat dramatically faster or classes overwhelmingly complex. New mechanics should support the game's design pillars and the sense of preparing for encounters in the world.

Specializations and talent respecs

  • Dual Specialization is planned to be available at level 40 for a cost.
  • Blizzard said it has no current plans to go beyond dual specialization.
  • Respec costs are intended to make talent choices meaningful; the temporary beta discount should not be treated as the live-game rule.

Balance expectations

Blizzard said it expects frequent class balance updates during the first six months after launch, initially aiming for a weekly cadence as performance data accumulates. The longer-term goal is to make relatively few design changes once the game is live and stable.

Practical takeaway: test your role and core rotation in beta, but avoid investing heavily based on one temporary build or one week of tuning.
