AzerothMarket expands its WoW Forever information hub

Published September 16, 2026 · Updated September 16, 2026

AzerothMarket has expanded its World of Warcraft: Forever section into a connected information hub covering the areas users are most likely to research together: gold, professions, items, equipment, dungeons and raids, guides and market information.

The goal is to make each topic understandable on its own while preserving clear links between related entities. For example, a profession can lead to its materials, those materials can lead to item pages, and item or equipment pages can lead to the relevant dungeon, guide and market context.

What is now connected?

Gold · Professions · Items · Equipment · Dungeons & Raids · Guides · Market

What happens next?

New guides, database entries and dated updates can be added continuously. Time-sensitive information will carry publication and update dates, while evergreen reference pages will remain focused on durable explanations and entity relationships.
