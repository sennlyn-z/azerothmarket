WoW Forever Live Dungeon Leveling Guide

Understand the November 9 dungeon creature XP increase and how to evaluate dungeon runs without relying on unsupported XP-per-hour claims.

Live-game update: November 15, 2026. Blizzard's November 9 hotfix increased experience earned from killing dungeon creatures by 50%. This is a confirmed change to creature-kill XP, not a guarantee that every dungeon run or every character will level 50% faster.

What changed

The hotfix states that experience from dungeon creature kills increased by 50%. The practical value of a run still depends on your level, group pace, downtime, creature kills, dungeon availability and whether you complete quests alongside the run.

How to use dungeons while leveling

  1. Check the level range and available dungeon queue or group options in your current Ruleset.
  2. Compare the time spent traveling, forming a group and clearing with the experience earned from the actual run.
  3. Complete relevant dungeon quests when available, but verify each quest's current reward in the live client.
  4. Keep group pace and completion reliability in mind; a slower run can erase the advantage of higher creature XP.
  5. Reassess after level changes because creature experience and group efficiency can vary across level ranges.

What we are not claiming

  • No universal best dungeon route is asserted here.
  • No exact XP-per-hour figure is published without reproducible live-game measurements.
  • The 50% adjustment is not described as a 50% increase to quest XP, boss loot or all dungeon rewards.

Hotfix context: Blizzard also listed dungeon and loot fixes in the November 9 update. Encounter details and loot tables should be checked separately rather than inferred from the XP adjustment.
