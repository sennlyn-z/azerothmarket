Live Hotfix Roundup · November 6–13, 2026

WoW Forever Hotfixes: November 6–13, 2026

A dated summary of live-game class, dungeon, profession and economy adjustments published during the first full weeks after launch.

Coverage window: November 6–13, 2026. This summary records live-game hotfixes announced by Blizzard. It is not a complete list of every minor bug fix, and the game client remains authoritative for current behavior.

November 13 — combat and class fixes

  • Player-versus-creature stun diminishing-return behavior was corrected in both directions.
  • Restoration Druid Wild Growth healing coefficient increased from 3.3% to 6.6%; a separate fix corrected its moving-cast behavior.
  • Mage Frost Nova now breaks correctly from proc and Blizzard damage.
  • Paladin Judgement of Command now activates on-hit effects and counts as a melee attack; other Judgements continue to count as ranged attacks.

November 11 — class behavior

  • Feral Druid Rend and Tear now works with the Pounce bleed.
  • Hunter Volley received fixes.
  • Paladin Judgement classification and Retribution Unsealed Fury interactions were corrected.
  • Priest Gnome Confounding Flash can be used in Shadowform.

November 10 — economy, mail and class tuning

  • Auction House minimum deposit changed from 1 silver to 1 copper.
  • Mail sender display and delivery timing issues were fixed.
  • Most quests at level 48 and above now grant additional gold. Blizzard noted that selected quests are excluded.
  • Hunter pet health was corrected from 150% to 115%; Arcane Shot ranks use 11% base mana, and Beast Mastery Hawk pet melee damage was increased.
  • Shaman weapon imbues can coexist with weapon totems; additional Enhancement and Warlock adjustments were included.
  • Profession-related adjustments included lower Minor Arcane Elixir vendor value, Blacksmithing and Cooking skill requirement changes, an Enchanting rod requirement for Arcane Salvager, lower Iron Grenade vendor pricing, and removal of Bind-on-Pickup from Swiftstrike Shot and Swiftfeather Arrow.

November 9 — dungeon experience

  • Experience from killing dungeon creatures increased by 50%.
  • Blizzard also corrected dungeon encounters, loot and creature behavior, including fixes affecting Alcaz Prison, Blackrock Depths and The Drowned City.

November 6 — early live fixes

  • Class, quest, interface, NPC, dungeon and item fixes were published.
  • Leatherworking Tanning Rack skill requirement was reduced from 140 to 125.

Interpretation: the 50% dungeon-creature XP change applies to creature kills in dungeons; it should not be read as a blanket 50% increase to all dungeon rewards or all leveling XP.
