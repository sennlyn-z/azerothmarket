Beta Known Issues: September 18

September 18, 2026 · Official Blizzard issue list summary

Blizzard's September 18 known-issues post lists a number of problems still being worked on in the World of Warcraft: Forever beta. The list is a testing snapshot and can change with later beta builds.

Guild and interface issues

  • Guild charter signatures were reported as failing in the beta, with a related zero-signature hand-in issue under investigation.
  • Barber Chairs can cause a LUA error.
  • The minimap may appear partially off screen.
  • Character friends are disabled in the beta.

Gameplay issues

  • Waylaid Crate behavior has specific item-stack edge cases, with a fix targeted for the next beta build.
  • The Cooldown Manager remains a work in progress.
  • Several class and combat interactions are being corrected in future builds.
Important: “Fixed in the next Beta build” means Blizzard has identified a target fix; it does not mean the fix is already live.

Source: Blizzard's official WoW Forever Beta Known Issues post.
