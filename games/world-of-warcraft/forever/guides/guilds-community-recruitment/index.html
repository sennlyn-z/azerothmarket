WoW Forever Guilds & Communities: Finding the Right Recruitment Post

How to use dated, public recruitment information to compare guild region, faction, playstyle, language and activity without treating a listing as a guarantee.

Use public recruitment information

AzerothMarket's guild directory is a community resource. Listings are sourced from public recruitment posts and should be treated as snapshots that can become outdated.

Useful filters

  • Region and likely play schedule.
  • Faction and preferred playstyle.
  • PvE, PvP or social focus.
  • Language and community location.
  • Recruitment date and original source.

Always open the original recruitment post before joining. AzerothMarket does not endorse individual guilds or guarantee that a listing remains active.
