City of Dalaran Dungeon: WoW Forever Beta Checklist

A practical checklist for testing the new City of Dalaran dungeon and interpreting the October 8 XP changes without confusing confirmed notes with community speculation.

Confirmed in the October 8 build

  • City of Dalaran became available for beta testing.
  • XP from killing dungeon creatures increased by approximately 20%.
  • A party XP issue involving higher-level players killing lower-level enemies was fixed.
  • Classic looting behavior was restored for creatures that drop no loot.
  • Fixes addressed visual flickering on large creatures and unintended long-distance pulls.
  • Weapon swaps within the same global cooldown were restored for supported cases.

How to test dungeon XP responsibly

  1. Record the date/build, character level, party composition, and dungeon.
  2. Compare similar runs and similar enemy types; do not compare solo and group results as if conditions were identical.
  3. Track creature-kill XP separately from quest rewards. The published ~20% change refers to creature kills, not a universal increase to every XP source.
  4. Expect tuning to change before launch.

Questions still worth verifying

  • Does faction access to Dalaran persist after later builds?
  • Which NPC services are usable after the quest sequence?
  • Do party composition or instance changes affect the run you are measuring?

Reports about Horde access and the city’s lore implications are still beta observations and discussion, not a confirmed permanent faction rule.

Source: Blizzard forums — WoW Forever Beta Development Notes, October 8, 2026.
