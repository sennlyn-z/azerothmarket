What changes

More recipes. More utility. More reasons to craft.

600+ new recipes

Blizzard says professions receive more than 600 new recipes spread across professions and level ranges, expanding the crafting loop throughout the 1–60 journey.

Food updates

Crafted food is adjusted to provide a small experience bonus and stat boosts, giving cooking a clearer role during progression.

Dungeon Blueprints

Advanced Blueprint recipes can drop from specific dungeon bosses, linking profession progression to group content.

Camping

Basic Campfires turn crafting into a social system.

A Basic Campfire creates a campsite where players can rest, access vendors, repair equipment, use profession workspaces and receive one-hour buffs. Nearby players can contribute crafted objects; each profession has its own utility, and more advanced campsite objects come from Blueprint recipes.

Examples announced by Blizzard include Blacksmithing's Sharpening Wheel, Tailoring's Faction Banner, Herbalism's Incense Candle, Alchemy Labs and Leatherworking's Tanning Rack. Cooking can unlock upgraded campfires that support additional objects.
