World of Warcraft • Forever

A new permanent Azeroth, built around the journey.

World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard's new permanent home for a reimagined original Azeroth. It uses the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor as the foundation, begins after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core, and centers progression around the level 1–60 journey, exploration, social play and future expansion.

What is Forever?

Built beside modern WoW and Classic, not as another seasonal mode.

Blizzard describes Forever as a new permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW. The design puts the world itself at the center: leveling matters, rewards come from the journey, and players are encouraged to explore, prepare, communicate and cooperate rather than rush only toward endgame.

The journey

Azeroth expands without abandoning its original geography.

Eastern Kingdoms & Kalimdor

Familiar continents receive new and reimagined regions, unfinished corners and more than 1,000 new quests woven through the level 1–60 experience.

New frontiers

Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, Riverglades and Zephras Isle add new stories and exploration opportunities to the original-world foundation.

Skyborne

A new playable race begins on Zephras Isle. Horde Skyborne can choose Shaman; Alliance Skyborne can choose Mage. Both factions also have Warrior, Hunter, Rogue and Druid options.
