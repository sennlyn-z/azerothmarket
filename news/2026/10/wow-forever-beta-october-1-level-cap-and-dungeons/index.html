WoW Forever Beta Raises Level Cap to 30 and Opens More Dungeons

The October 1 development update expanded the beta's leveling and dungeon test range, including a level-cap increase and additional dungeon availability.

Blizzard's October 1 development notes raised the beta level cap to 30. The update also made the Excavation Site: Wetlands available for levels 26–31, alongside Razorfen Downs (25+) and Uldaman (30+).

How to use this information

These level ranges describe the beta notes at the time of publication; they should not be interpreted as a guarantee of final tuning or recommended group composition. Check current notes before planning a route around a particular dungeon.
