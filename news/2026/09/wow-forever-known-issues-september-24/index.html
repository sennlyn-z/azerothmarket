WoW Forever Known Issues Updated September 24

Blizzard updated the WoW Forever beta known-issues list with tracking problems for cooldowns, curses, item enhancements, the minimap and party world instances.

Blizzard published an updated WoW Forever beta known-issues list on September 24. The update includes several interface and tracking problems that remain under investigation.

Newly listed issues

  • Banes and Curses may not track correctly after changing targets.
  • Healthstone, pet and several other cooldowns cannot currently be tracked correctly.
  • Arcane Blast and some item enhancements cannot be tracked.
  • Stormwind's minimap has display issues.
  • Personal Resource Display does not show combo points.
  • Changing party composition may move players to a different world instance.

These are beta issues, not permanent descriptions of the launch game. Blizzard's wording should be used as the source of truth for whether an item is resolved or still under investigation.
