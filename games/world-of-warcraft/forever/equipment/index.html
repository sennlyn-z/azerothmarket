World of Warcraft: Forever

Equipment becomes a series of meaningful choices.

Forever's itemization updates extend from trinkets and set bonuses to dungeon drops, quest rewards and world loot. The goal announced by Blizzard is broader, more varied gear while preserving the excitement of memorable finds.

Itemization

Gear changes announced by Blizzard.

Trinkets

Trinkets are being pushed toward more varied, situational effects, including effects tied to environments or creature types.

Set bonuses

Set bonuses are being improved so that collecting and completing sets provides more useful gameplay value.

Dungeon loot

Every dungeon drop has been reviewed, with less exciting items adjusted and hundreds of new drops added.

Quest equipment

Familiar quests can offer additional item choices to support more classes and roles.

Rare & world drops

Hundreds of rare-creature drops and improved world-drop epics add more reasons to explore outside standard dungeon routes.

Blue boss items

Unique dungeon boss items are now blue quality, helping their special identity stand out in the progression flow.

Economy & demand

Equipment information that can become market intelligence.

AzerothMarket will track equipment by source, rarity, role, usefulness and acquisition difficulty. As the live economy develops, these attributes can support more useful research pages and price context.
