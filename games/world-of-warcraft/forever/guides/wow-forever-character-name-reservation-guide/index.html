WoW Forever Character Name Reservation Guide

A practical guide to Blizzard's early character-creation window, eligibility rules, character limit and the two-part naming system.

Reservation window: October 27 through November 3, 2026. Blizzard's announced window is currently underway as of this guide's update date. Availability is first-come, first-served; a preferred name is not guaranteed.

1. Who can reserve names?

Blizzard's announcement says access is available to eligible players who purchase the WoW: Forever Digital Upgrade or Collector's Edition. It also lists pre-purchase of the Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack or the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection as qualifying access routes. Check the official product page and your Battle.net account before purchasing anything; benefits and eligibility should be confirmed directly with Blizzard.

2. How many characters can you create?

  • You can create and reserve up to three characters total across all Rulesets.
  • Characters cannot enter the game world until the November 4 launch.
  • Names are allocated first-come, first-served, so prepare alternatives.

3. Understand the two-part name system

Forever characters use a first name and surname. Blizzard's naming explanation says the full first-name-plus-surname combination must be unique within a region. This means another character may share your first name if the complete combination differs.

4. Battle.net preparation

  1. Sign in to the Battle.net account that has the qualifying purchase or access.
  2. Open the World of Warcraft game version selector and choose WoW: Forever.
  3. Install or update the client if prompted.
  4. Open character creation, select your Ruleset and create up to three characters.
  5. Confirm each full name and Ruleset before finalizing. Keep a written list of backup first-name/surname combinations.

5. Regional start times announced by Blizzard

  • Europe: October 27, 18:00 CET.
  • North America: October 27, 1:00 p.m. PDT.
  • Korea: October 28, 7:00 a.m. KST.
  • Taiwan: October 28, 7:00 a.m. CST.

The reservation period ends November 3. This guide is a dated summary; if the client, account page or official announcement shows different eligibility or timing, follow Blizzard's current instructions.
