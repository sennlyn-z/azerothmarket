WoW Forever Is Live: November 4 Launch Overview

World of Warcraft: Forever has reached its announced November 4 launch. Here is a practical launch-day overview and the key details players should verify in the live client.

Launch status: Blizzard announced WoW: Forever's global launch for November 4 at 3:00 p.m. Pacific. This article records the launch milestone; it does not independently verify realm availability or current server status.

What to do first

  1. Open Battle.net and confirm that the World of Warcraft: Forever client is selected and fully updated.
  2. Review the available Rulesets before creating or entering a character. Your choice can affect the gameplay environment.
  3. Check your character's name, race, class and intended role. Name reservations were a pre-launch window and are now part of launch preparation history.
  4. Use the in-game help and official support channels for account, queue, or client issues.

What comes next?

Blizzard's published roadmap points to the first new raid unlock on December 9, followed by additional phases of dungeons, raids, PvP, world content and quest progression. Treat roadmap dates as announced milestones and check Blizzard's current posts for changes.
