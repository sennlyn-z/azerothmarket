WoW Forever Beta Gets September 24 Update

Blizzard deployed a new WoW Forever beta build on September 24 with bug fixes affecting casting, Campfire buffs, models and other systems.

Blizzard deployed a new WoW Forever beta build on September 24, 2026. The official development notes describe a set of bug fixes rather than a new content release.

Campfire behavior fixed

One notable fix changes the experience while waiting to obtain a Campfire buff. Players can now buff each other, cast spells and craft while waiting. Blizzard also says players can obtain the buff while sitting, sitting in a chair, sleeping, lying down or using the kneeling emote.

Other fixes

The update also addresses caster precast animations, the water-jumping sound effect, Human death sounds, a rendering issue affecting Huey Sunnydale's legs and an issue that exposed a Season of Discovery rune ability.
