PvP Progression in WoW Forever: What to Verify During Beta

Use this checklist to follow reported PvP system changes without treating early coverage as a final in-game guide.

What recent reporting describes

PC Gamer has reported a progression design involving Honor currency, weekly capped Rank points, 14 reward tiers, seasonal progression and Legacy Points. Treat this as reported design context and verify implementation in current official notes and the game itself.

Questions to confirm before making a build or reward guide

  • How is Honor earned, and what can it purchase?
  • When are Rank points awarded or reset?
  • Which rewards are tied to each tier?
  • How do seasons and Legacy Points interact?
  • Have any values changed since the source was published?

Keep guides versioned

Include the observation date, build or source link, and a clear distinction between confirmed rules and community interpretation. Update the guide when official documentation changes.
