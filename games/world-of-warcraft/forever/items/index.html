World of Warcraft: Forever

Items are part of the journey.

Blizzard is revisiting the itemization of original Azeroth: every dungeon drop has been reviewed, hundreds of new drops have been added, quest rewards have expanded, and rare-creature loot and world-drop epics are being improved.

Loot philosophy

A broader item pool without losing memorable rewards.

Dungeon drops

Every dungeon drop has been re-examined. Less exciting items can be adjusted while memorable items remain meaningful.

Hundreds of new drops

New dungeon and world loot expands the number of interesting items players can discover while leveling and grouping.

Quest rewards

Quest rewards are being expanded to better support different classes and roles, including new alternatives on familiar quests.

Rare creatures

Hundreds of new rare-creature drops add exploration-based targets and more reasons to investigate the open world.

World-drop epics

World-drop epics are being improved as part of the broader effort to make item discovery more varied.

Dungeon boss items

Unique dungeon boss items are now blue quality, while set bonuses have been improved to feel more useful.

Market angle

Which items can matter to an economy?

AzerothMarket will organize Forever items around the factors players actually research: source, rarity, usefulness, acquisition route, profession interaction and demand. Actual transaction quotes will be handled through live support and can change with supply and demand.
