# AzerothMarket — Content Rules

## Voice
- Clear, factual, useful, and concise.
- Professional but not corporate or overly formal.
- Write for players researching an economy, item, system, or market decision.
- Avoid empty marketing language.

## Answer-first rule
Start pages with a short explanation of what the user is looking at and why it matters. Put deeper details afterward.

## Game-specific information
For World of Warcraft: Forever, distinguish confirmed game facts from estimates, community observations, or market commentary. When a value can change, show the relevant date or update context.

## Market information
- Do not invent live prices, supply, demand, listings, or transaction data.
- If live data is unavailable, say so clearly and provide the useful static context that remains valid.
- Keep market content connected to items, gold, equipment, professions, and guides.

## Entity pages
A strong entity page should answer, as applicable:
- What is it?
- Where is it obtained?
- What is it used for?
- What profession/recipe is related?
- What equipment or content is related?
- What gold or market relevance does it have?
- What should the reader visit next?

## Dates
For news and other changing content, use visible Published / Updated information. Do not imply current information is timeless.

## Linking
Every substantial new page should link to relevant parent/child entities. Avoid orphan content.

## Cleanup
Do not add duplicate pages merely for alternate keywords. Expand an existing hub when it already owns the topic. Delete obsolete pages only after checking navigation, sitemap, and inbound links.

## Future expansion
The same rules should apply if AzerothMarket expands beyond WoW Forever: preserve the global design system while giving each game its own clean entity hierarchy.
