WoW Forever RMT Warning: Blizzard Says Illicit Gold Trading Can Lead to Bans

Players researching the Forever economy should distinguish legitimate in-game trade from prohibited real-money trading and illicit gold transfers.

GamesRadar reports that Blizzard has reiterated its stance against real-money trading (RMT) following beta enforcement actions involving botting and illicit gold. The report also notes warnings about accepting suspicious gold transfers from strangers.

Practical safety guidance

  • Do not buy or sell gold, items or services for real money when prohibited by the game's rules.
  • Avoid third-party offers that ask for account credentials, payment outside trusted channels or unusual transfers.
  • When a trade seems suspicious, pause rather than accepting assets simply because they are offered for free.
  • Check Blizzard's current policies and announcements directly; enforcement details may evolve.
