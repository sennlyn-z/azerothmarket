World of Warcraft: Forever

New dungeons. New raids. More reasons to group.

Forever expands the original-world dungeon and raid loop with nine new dungeons, two announced raid experiences, and a new 15 vs 15 battleground in the Darkspear Islands.

Raids

Endgame group content announced for Forever.

Barrow Deeps

A planned maximum-level 10-player raid challenge with new encounters, tier sets and a legendary reward.

Hyjal Summit

A 20-player raid experience set around one of Azeroth's most important locations.

Roadmap

New raids unlock December 9, 2026, with further raids and dungeons planned beyond launch.

PvP

Darkspear Islands

Blizzard announced a 15 vs 15 battleground off the coast of Kalimdor, combining strategic control points with Arathi Basin-style flag capture and defense.

Economy angle

Dungeons connect directly to the Forever economy.

Dungeons are not only combat content. Bosses can supply advanced profession Blueprints, unique blue-quality items and other loot that feeds the crafting and equipment economy.
