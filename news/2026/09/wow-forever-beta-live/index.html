World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Is Now Live

September 17, 2026 · Updated September 20, 2026

World of Warcraft: Forever entered beta on September 17, 2026. Blizzard says the beta is available through October 21, with the full game scheduled to launch globally on November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST.

Current status: Beta is live. Blizzard is actively addressing launch-period technical issues while expanding server capacity and monitoring the test environment.
