Mail Delivery Video Requirements

If an order requires a mail-delivery recording, the video should clearly show the account region, the selected ruleset, the recipient character ID and the gold transfer result.

Recording principle
Keep the relevant steps visible and in sequence. The recording should make it possible to match the game account, ruleset, recipient and delivered amount to the order details.
01
Battle.net account region

Show the Battle.net client first

Begin the delivery recording by switching to the Battle.net client. Make sure the region displayed in the upper-right corner matches the game region specified in the order.

Check: The account region shown in the client matches the region on the order.
02
Game ruleset

Confirm the correct ruleset at the login screen

At the game login screen, open the Ruleset selector at the top. Choose the mode specified in the order and confirm that the character being used for delivery belongs to the correct ruleset.

Check: The selected mode and the delivery character's ruleset match the order.
03
Recipient and amount

Show the mail recipient and gold amounts

Open the in-game mail sending interface. Confirm that the recipient character ID is correct. While entering the ID, keep the relevant details visible: the recipient ID, the remaining amount of gold in the sender's bags, and the amount of gold being sent. These amounts should match the order.

Check: Recipient character ID, remaining bag gold and mail amount are visible and consistent with the order.
04
Successful delivery

Show the “Mail sent” confirmation

The mail must not be sent as a “Cash on Delivery” transaction (that is, a payment-required-on-receipt mail). The recording must show the successful sending confirmation, “Mail sent”, and show that the gold amount in the sender's bags has decreased.

Check: The video shows “Mail sent” and the corresponding reduction in the sender's bag gold.

Quick recording checklist

  • Battle.net region matches the order's game region.
  • Selected ruleset and delivery character are correct.
  • Recipient character ID is clearly visible and correct.
  • Remaining bag gold and sent amount match the order.
  • The mail is not Cash on Delivery.
  • “Mail sent” confirmation and reduced bag gold are visible.
