World of Warcraft · Forever

A reimagined Azeroth, built to last.

World of Warcraft: Forever is Blizzard's new permanent game experience built around original Azeroth. It expands the familiar Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor with new regions, quests, dungeons, systems and future content while keeping the world, social play and the level 1–60 journey at the center.

What is Forever?

A new branch of Warcraft, not just another season.

Blizzard describes Forever as a new permanent home alongside modern World of Warcraft and Classic WoW. It is set after the events of Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before the era represented by Molten Core, returning players to the original continents for a fresh level 1–60 adventure. Blizzard has also said Forever is not a temporary mode, a seasonal ruleset, or simply another version of Classic; the intent is to keep expanding the world over time.

Core pillars

The design philosophy

Classic foundations with new content.

01

World first

The world itself remains the focus, with exploration, travel, discovery and chance encounters carrying real weight.

02

Journey matters

Leveling is treated as part of the experience rather than merely a bridge to an endgame destination.

03

Social by design

Classic-style cooperation, communication and recognizable player communities are central to the experience.

04

Approachable & familiar

New systems are measured against the tone, pacing and identity of original Azeroth.

The world

Eastern Kingdoms & Kalimdor, opened up again.

Level 1–60 remains the foundation.

New & reimagined regions

More than a thousand new quests

Forever expands the classic continents with new and reimagined regions, unfinished corners of the world, characters, stories and quests. Blizzard says the first journey contains more than 1,000 new quests woven through the level 1–60 experience.

Travel

No flying mounts

Travel remains grounded. Blizzard says there will be no flying mounts in Forever, reinforcing the importance of roads, routes and outdoor exploration.

Scaling

No level scaling

Zones and enemies are not being normalized to your character level, preserving a more classic sense of danger, preparation and progression.

Systems

New systems that change the everyday journey.

Camping

Turn a campfire into a social hub.

The new Camping system expands the simple Cooking Fire into a campsite where players can rest and gain useful services and buffs. Nearby players can also contribute profession-crafted objects to the camp.

Legacy

Progression for alts and long-term play.

Legacy progression is designed for players who enjoy leveling and maintaining alternate characters, adding a longer-term layer to the 1–60 journey.

Professions

More reasons to craft and gather.

Blizzard has outlined more than 600 new recipes across professions and level ranges, along with campsite utility, crafted food updates and advanced Blueprint recipes tied to dungeon bosses.

Transmog

Opt-in customization.

Players can use optional transmog plus modern or Classic visual presets, with HD or Standard-Definition character models and restored Classic animations for HD models.

Controls

Official gamepad support.

Forever will support official gamepad play for players who prefer a controller setup.

Combat

Classic-minded pacing.

The design keeps the deliberate feel of Classic combat, where individual pulls, threat, crowd control, preparation and class identity continue to matter.

Playable races & rulesets

More ways to shape your character's journey.

New race

Skyborne

The Skyborne are a new playable race introduced with Forever. Optional upgrade editions provide access to the race and its Zephras Isle starting experience ahead of launch.

Rulesets

Choose your style of Azeroth.

Blizzard's current plans include distinct ecosystems for standard play, PvP, and Roleplaying, with Hardcore arriving after launch. Players within the same ruleset can group, dungeon and raid together.

Hardcore

High-stakes progression.

Hardcore is planned as a separate ecosystem after launch. Characters that die can move to another ruleset, while Hardcore progression remains isolated from ordinary ruleset progression.

Dungeons, raids & future content

The launch is the beginning, not the endpoint.

Launch journey

Explore first, then go deeper.

Forever starts with the level 1–60 open-world journey across original continents, new regions and unfinished corners, with dungeon and raid progression expanding after launch.

Road ahead

New raids, dungeons and world content.

Blizzard has announced additional raids, dungeons, PvP updates, world content, more quests and a revamped iconic raid as part of the ongoing roadmap.

Long-term

Designed as an evergreen world.

The stated goal is continued development guided by community feedback rather than a one-off Classic release.
