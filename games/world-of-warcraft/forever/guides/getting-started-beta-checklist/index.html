Getting Started

WoW Forever Beta Getting Started Checklist

A practical checklist for preparing a character, testing systems, recording discoveries, and separating beta information from confirmed launch facts.

Start with verified systems

The Forever beta is a testing environment. Use it to learn the shape of the game, but keep a clear distinction between current beta behavior, announced launch plans, and values that may still change.

Before you invest time

  • Confirm the current beta build and date.
  • Record which systems are officially announced versus observed in-game.
  • Keep notes on quests, professions, items and dungeon discoveries with their source.
  • Do not treat temporary bugs or placeholder values as permanent mechanics.

Build your personal knowledge map

Connect your leveling route to zones and quests, then connect professions to materials and gold. Dungeon runs should be tracked through bosses, loot and equipment rather than isolated item lists.
