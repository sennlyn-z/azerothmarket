WoW Forever Hotfix Tracker

A chronological record of confirmed live-game hotfixes, designed to keep dated changes separate and easy to verify.

Live tracker · Last reviewed November 15, 2026. This page consolidates dated WoW Forever hotfix changes covered by AzerothMarket. Each entry retains its original date so older tuning is not mistaken for the current state.

November 13, 2026

  • Stun diminishing-return behavior corrected for player-versus-creature combat.
  • Restoration Druid Wild Growth coefficient increased from 3.3% to 6.6%; moving-cast behavior corrected.
  • Mage Frost Nova break behavior fixed.
  • Paladin Judgement of Command now triggers on-hit effects and counts as melee; other Judgements remain ranged.

November 11, 2026

  • Feral Druid Rend and Tear interaction with Pounce bleed fixed.
  • Hunter Volley fixes; Paladin Judgement and Unsealed Fury behavior adjusted; Priest Gnome Confounding Flash usable in Shadowform.

November 10, 2026

  • Auction House minimum deposit: 1 silver → 1 copper.
  • Most level 48+ quests now grant additional gold, with selected exclusions.
  • Mail display and delivery timing fixes.
  • Hunter pet health corrected to 115%; Arcane Shot ranks standardized to 11% base mana; Beast Mastery Hawk pet melee damage increased.
  • Shaman weapon imbue/totem coexistence and additional class adjustments.
  • Profession, vendor-value, item-price and ammunition binding changes; details in the profession update.

November 9, 2026

  • Dungeon creature kill experience increased by 50%.
  • Dungeon, encounter and loot fixes.

November 6, 2026

  • Class, interface, NPC, quest, dungeon and item fixes.
  • Leatherworking Tanning Rack requirement reduced from 140 to 125.

How to read this tracker

Dates refer to Blizzard's hotfix publication dates. A listed adjustment may be superseded by a later hotfix. We summarize the announced changes and avoid extrapolating unlisted class, loot or economy effects.

For the most current implementation details, check Blizzard's official hotfix posts and the live game client. This tracker is a secondary summary, not an official patch note archive.
