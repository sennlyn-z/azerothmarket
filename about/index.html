About AzerothMarket

Information first. Human support when it matters.

AzerothMarket is being built as a structured information and market platform for online game economies. We organize currencies, items, equipment, professions, guides and market context so users can research before they trade.

Research

Structured pages designed to answer specific questions clearly and connect related entities.

Freshness

News and updates are published with dates so changing information can be distinguished from evergreen guides.

Transparency

We avoid invented reviews, fake statistics and unsupported claims. Information pages are separated from live transaction quotes.
