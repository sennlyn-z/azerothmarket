WoW Forever Profession Changes: November 2026

A reference to the confirmed profession-related live hotfixes, with practical notes for crafting and economy research.

Live-game update: November 15, 2026. The November 6 and November 10 hotfixes changed several profession requirements, vendor values and item binding rules. The notes below record the announced changes; recipe availability and current skill-up behavior should still be checked in game.

Confirmed profession changes

  • Leatherworking: Tanning Rack skill requirement reduced from 140 to 125 (November 6).
  • Blacksmithing: Coarse Grinding Stone now grants skill-ups through 90, previously 75 (November 10).
  • Cooking: Journeyman Campfire Kit requirement reduced from 140 to 90 (November 10).
  • Enchanting: Arcane Salvager now requires a Runed Golden Rod (November 10).
  • Engineering: Iron Grenades vendor price reduced to 1 silver 25 copper (November 10).
  • Items and ammunition: Swiftstrike Shot and Swiftfeather Arrow are no longer Bind on Pickup (November 10).
  • Alchemy/vendor value: Minor Arcane Elixir vendor sell value reduced from 25 copper to 5 copper (November 10).

What this means for profession planning

  • Recheck the live skill-up range before stocking materials for a leveling session.
  • Revisit the cost of Engineering crafts using Iron Grenades; the vendor-price adjustment changes the known baseline, but does not establish an Auction House market price.
  • Check whether the revised binding status changes who can trade or list the ammunition in your Ruleset.
  • Do not treat vendor sell value as a player-market valuation.

AzerothMarket does not publish unverified recipe costs, Auction House prices or profit margins. Those require current realm/Ruleset data and market observations.
