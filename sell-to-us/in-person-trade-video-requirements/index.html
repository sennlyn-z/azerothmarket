In-Person Trade Video Requirements

For an in-person character-to-character trade, record the complete process so the recipient, traded gold amount, successful completion, selected ruleset and account region can be checked against the order.

Recording principle
Start recording before the trade begins. Keep the entire trade process visible without cuts, and make sure the success confirmation and the subsequent account and ruleset checks are included.
01
Find the recipient

Start recording and locate the receiving character

Begin the video recording, then find the recipient character in the game world. Make sure the character receiving the trade can be identified before opening the trade window.

Check: The recording starts before the transaction and clearly shows the intended receiving character.
02
Record the full trade

Capture every step of the trade window

Record the entire trade process. Add the amount of gold specified in the order. It is also recommended to include a few low-value, ordinary items in the trade window as additional visible trade contents. Both players must review and confirm the trade. After confirmation, the video must show the in-game message indicating that the trade was successful.

Check: The full trade is visible, the gold amount matches the order, both sides confirm, and the successful-trade message appears.
03
Verify the ruleset

Return to the character-selection screen

After the trade, log out of the character to return to the character-selection screen. Open the Ruleset selector, choose or display the relevant ruleset, and confirm that it is the ruleset specified in the order.

Check: The ruleset shown on the character-selection screen matches the order.
04
Verify the account region

Show the Battle.net client region

Switch to the Battle.net client and show the account region displayed in the lower-left corner. Confirm that this region matches the game region specified in the order.

Check: The region shown in the lower-left corner of the Battle.net client matches the order's game region.

Quick recording checklist

  • Recording starts before locating and trading with the receiving character.
  • The entire trade process is captured without missing steps.
  • The gold amount matches the order; a few low-value items are recommended as additional trade contents.
  • Both players confirm the trade.
  • The game displays a successful-trade message.
  • The character-selection screen shows the correct ruleset.
  • The Battle.net client shows the correct account region in the lower-left corner.
